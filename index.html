Who Shares a Ride?

One day of taxi requests in Thessaloniki. Requests made in the same 5 minutes were paired into shared trips when the detour was small enough. Each shared trip serves at most two passengers.

Layers

Click a shared trip to see its two requests and how much distance sharing saved. Solid circles are pick-ups and hollow circles are drop-offs.

All locations are anonymized. Every pick-up and drop-off was moved 150 to 500 m from its real position and all routes were recalculated on the road network, so the figures are for demonstration only.

From the diploma thesis Shared-mobility services and shared-taxi optimization, Aristotle University of Thessaloniki, by Giorgos Iliopoulos.
